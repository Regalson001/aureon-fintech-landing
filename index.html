GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is AUREON a real trading or brokerage service? +

No. AUREON is a fictional front-end portfolio concept. It does not provide brokerage, investment, custody or financial services.

Are the market prices and account fees live? +

No. All prices, spreads, fees, performance figures and platform screens are illustrative placeholders for demonstrating interface design.

Does this website execute trades or collect payments? +

No. The interface does not connect to an exchange, broker, payment provider or trading API. The contact form is a front-end demonstration only.
